OVERVIEW

What this module does for you

As an RMDO you ask for publicity material for your HQ. Managers approve it, the head office orders it from the vendor, and you confirm what arrived. Every step is tracked, so you always know where your request is.

You start itRaise a request with the items, sizes and quantities.
Five approvalsRM, SMD, SMM, ADMIN-MD CS and DGM-MD CS check it.
You close itAcknowledge the delivery, including short or damaged items.

PROBLEMS

Problems and answers

I submitted, and now there is no pencil icon
After you submit, only an approver can return the request to you. Once it shows Returned, the pencil appears again.
I see "No requests found"
Your filters may be too narrow. Press Clear All.
Some items arrived damaged
Enter the received and damaged quantities when you acknowledge. The request becomes Exception raised and head office sends the rest.
Who has my request right now?
Open the request. The timeline shows each approver and who has it now.
